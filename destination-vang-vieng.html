Vang Vieng

The adventure capital of Laos, where dramatic karst landscapes meet serene river life.

Blessed with tall, majestic mountains, clear rivers, and glorious caves, Vang Vieng is a small town located north of Vientiane. Possessing a scenic view, it is the ideal vacation spot to unwind. Apart from the gorgeous landscape, the town offers many adventurous and thrilling activities to partake in. Despite its former reputation as a party town for young western travellers, the town is now an ecotourism hot spot thanks to its incredible jungles, mountains and rivers.

MULTI-SPORT PROGRAMS CHALLENGES CULTURAL IMMERSION FOOD DISCOVERY COOKING ZIP LINNIG VIA FERRATA ABSAILING TREKKING CYCLING

What to do in Vang Vieng

1. Journey along Famous Caves

Visit popular caves like Buddha Cave, Tham Chang, and Tham Nam filled with stalactites and history.

2. Kayaking in Nam Song river

Paddle through stunning limestone karsts on the gentle Nam Song river.

3. Cycling and Caving

Explore the surrounding countryside and hidden caves on two wheels.

4. Visit Blue Lagoons

Take a dip in the crystal clear turquoise waters of the famous lagoons.

5. Hot Air Balloon

Soar high for a panoramic view of the mountains at sunrise or sunset.

6. Rock Climbing

Challenge yourself on the numerous limestone karst mountains surrounding the town.

Vang Vieng by Night

Enjoy drinks with a one-of-a-kind sunset, shop at the Night Market for souvenirs, or experience the vibrant bar scene.
