Kong Lor Cave Expedition

Full Day • 7.5km Underground River Boat Ride

What Will You Do

We depart from The Rock View Point to Kong Lor Cave – a cave of mystery and legend. We will walk into the mouth of the cave and jump into our motorboat with a local boatman to cruise up the river that flows through the cave. This 7.5 km underground cruise passes through huge chambers filled with stalactites and stalagmites.

We exit the cave for a quick break in the small village on the other side before returning to boat for picnic lunch at river bank with amazing view of lime stone.

After our lunch, we start kayaking through and enjoy swimming at blue lagoon in front the cave, we will return to Phou Pha Marn before sunset.

Approximately: 2 hours driving, 1.5 hours Motorboat and 2 - 2.5 hours kayaking.

⚠️ What to Expect

Kayaking through the cave is not very technical but is done in areas of complete darkness.

Hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, comfortable trekking clothes, good quality trekking shoes, refillable water bottle, swimming clothes (including a sarong for women), towel.

💧 Our Water Policy

Help us to become 100% single use plastic free company!

We try to avoid plastic as much as possible, that is why we encourage you to bring your own refillable bottle. We have water refillable station at our office where you can fill up your bottle. If you don’t have one we sell the 0,75 L Laos Dream Destination aluminium bottles at our sales office. At the moment we will still need to use plastic bottles in some of our tours but we hope to reduce it as much as possible. Together with The Laos Dream Destination Sustainability Fund we are working on installing water filters in all the villages we are visiting and we hope in the future we will not need to use plastic bottles anymore.
